Tiling Adelaide

Tiling for Adelaide rooms, walls and finished surfaces.

Ellis Services Group discusses tiling projects for Adelaide bathrooms, kitchens and other interior or exterior finished surfaces. The best starting point is a clear description of the room, existing surface and the finish you are planning.

Bathroom tiling

Bathroom wall and floor tiling needs to be considered alongside the room layout, shower area, existing surfaces and project stage. If the bathroom is a wet-area project, review bathroom waterproofing Adelaide before discussing the final finish. Tell us whether the room is being renovated, repaired or prepared for new tiles.

Kitchen tiling and splashbacks

For a kitchen splashback or tiled kitchen surface, identify the wall or floor area, cabinetry and appliance stage, tile style if known, and whether existing finishes are still in place. A tile sample or a few photographs can make it easier to describe the look and practical surface you are planning.

Wall, floor and outdoor tiling

Wall tiles, internal floor tiles, courtyards and other outdoor surfaces can involve different visual and practical considerations. Start with the surface, approximate dimensions, existing condition and any planned transitions or edges. For outdoor or wet-exposed areas where waterproofing is part of the conversation, see waterproofing Adelaide.

Prepare a tiling enquiry

Before contacting Ellis Services Group, note the room or external area, the current surface, whether tiles are being replaced or added, and the intended finish. Include an Adelaide suburb or postcode; you can review Adelaide service areas before you send an enquiry.

Discuss tiling in Adelaide

Clear project information helps keep the first conversation focused on the actual room and finish. We can then discuss the appropriate next step without treating every tiled surface as the same scope.
