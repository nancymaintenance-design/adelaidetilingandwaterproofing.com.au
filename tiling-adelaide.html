Tiling Adelaide

Tiling for Adelaide rooms, walls and finished surfaces.

Ellis Services Group discusses tiling projects for Adelaide bathrooms, kitchens and other interior or exterior finished surfaces. The best starting point is a clear description of the room, existing surface and the finish you are planning.

Plan the surface before selecting the finish

Tile projects vary with the room and the surface beneath it. A bathroom wall, kitchen splashback, floor or courtyard may require different preparation and sequencing. Share the approximate area, tile type, existing finish and current project stage so the conversation is based on the actual space rather than a generic tile request.

If your project includes a bathroom wet area, read about bathroom waterproofing Adelaide. For broader wet-area planning before finished surfaces are installed, see our waterproofing Adelaide page.

Useful details for a tiling enquiry

It helps to know whether tiles are being added to a new surface, replacing an existing finish, or forming part of a renovation. Include the room, approximate dimensions, preferred tile material or format if known, and photographs of the current area. These details make it easier to discuss what the work may involve and which stage should happen next.

Coordinate tiling with wet-area work

In bathrooms and other wet areas, tiling is one part of the finished surface while waterproofing needs to be considered earlier in the project sequence. Keeping those topics on separate pages gives you a clearer path: this page focuses on tiling, while the waterproofing pages explain the related wet-area planning considerations.
