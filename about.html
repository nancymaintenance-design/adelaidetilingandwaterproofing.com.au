About Ellis Services Group: Adelaide Waterproofing & Tiling

Ellis Services Group delivers waterproofing, repairs and tiling services for Adelaide properties. We assess the room, surface condition and required finish, then set out the work required.

The company

One company for preparation, waterproofing and tile finishes

Ellis Services Group brings surface preparation, membrane installation and tile finishing into one agreed scope. For our customers, that means the base, waterproofing and finished surface are coordinated rather than arranged as separate jobs.

We service bathroom wet areas, laundries, tiled walls and floors, roofs, pools, balconies and other moisture-prone spaces across Adelaide.

Explore professional services for waterproofing, bathroom waterproofing and tiling, then call or send an enquiry with the details that matter to your project.

How an enquiry is arranged

How Ellis delivers your project

Our team checks the site, confirms the work in writing, completes the agreed installation and explains care for the finished surface.

01

Assess the property

We inspect the work area, take measurements and check the existing base, access and required finish.

02

Confirm the work

We identify preparation, materials, installation and timing in your written quote before booking.

03

Prepare and install

The written quote confirms the agreed scope, inclusions, timing and booking details before work begins. We carry out that scope with the curing and checks required between stages.

04

Finish and hand over

We explain the completed surfaces and provide care and return-to-use instructions for the installed materials.
