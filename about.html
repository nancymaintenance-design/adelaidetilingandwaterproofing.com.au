How an enquiry is arranged

Start with the space, then confirm the scope.

Good preparation makes it easier to discuss a wet-area or tiling project without assuming details that need to be checked on site or in the quote.

01

Describe the location

Share the Adelaide suburb, the room or external area, and whether the work concerns a bathroom, laundry, kitchen, wall, floor, balcony or another surface.

02

Explain the project stage

Tell us whether the area is being planned, stripped back, prepared, waterproofed, tiled or repaired. Mention existing conditions and any visible moisture concern.

03

Confirm before work begins

The written quote is where the scope, inclusions, timing and the contractor responsible for any regulated component should be confirmed before booking.

04

Confirm the next step

After the scope is clear, confirm availability, timing and the next action before booking.

Licences and insurance

Keep project-specific compliance clear.

Waterproofing, tiling and renovation work can involve different requirements depending on the property, scope and trade involved. A public ABN record does not state which licence class, insurance policy or level of cover applies to a particular project.

Before work begins, use the quote discussion to confirm the scope, the responsible contractor for any regulated work, and any licence or insurance evidence that is relevant to that job. This keeps the discussion specific to the actual work rather than making a broad claim that may not apply to every enquiry.

If your project includes another specialist trade, include that in the first enquiry so the right responsibility and sequence can be discussed before a booking is made.
