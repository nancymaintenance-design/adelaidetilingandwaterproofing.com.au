Balcony retiling, doorway transitions and exterior tile suitability

Balcony tiles need to work with the exposed base, drainage and doorway transition. We assess the balcony before planning tile removal or new installation, with attention to outlets, edges, movement junctions and the finished height at the door.

If water ponds or reaches the room below, another layer of tiles is not a diagnosis or a waterproofing repair. The existing build-up may need investigation and removal before the tiled finish can be agreed. Our balcony waterproofing service explains the connected membrane and drainage assessment.

For a strata property, share the authorised contact and access arrangements before booking. We describe the proposed work without deciding ownership boundaries or who must pay.

Balcony strip out and the membrane interface

Tile removal can damage a membrane that appears sound from above. We agree whether the existing tiled build-up is retained or removed, and where a waterproofing assessment is needed before installation. Covering a wet or unstable base with new tiles does not resolve the source. The interface between the prepared base, membrane and adhesive is confirmed as part of the connected work.

Door thresholds and outlet access

The tile thickness, adhesive bed and preparation affect the finished height at a door. We consider the available threshold, drainage direction and outlet access together before agreeing the finish. Coping and exposed edges also need a defined termination. Weather exposure and material curing affect access restrictions; the quote confirms the sequence rather than guaranteeing a fixed turnaround.

Balcony finish and drainage

Tell us about the existing tiles, doorway height and any ponding or leaks. We assess tile removal and the membrane interface together.

The installation scope includes the agreed preparation, exterior tile finish, outlets and edge details.
