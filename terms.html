Adelaide Waterproofing & Tiling Website Terms

This website describes the waterproofing and tiling services provided by Ellis Services Group across Adelaide. It helps you choose a service and send a project enquiry.

Service information and quotes

Website descriptions are general service information, not a project-specific quotation or construction specification. Our written quote confirms the work area, preparation, materials, inclusions, timing and price for your property. Any approved additional work is confirmed with you before proceeding.

Bookings and work scope

Submitting an enquiry starts the service enquiry process; it does not by itself confirm an appointment or authorise work. Booking, payment, cancellation and any warranty terms applicable to your project are set out in the agreed quote or contract. Your applicable consumer rights are not excluded by these website terms.

Images and external links

Service imagery illustrates types of surfaces and finishes and is not presented as a record of an Ellis project unless expressly identified as such. External links, including maps, social media and public business records, are provided for convenience and operate under their own terms.
