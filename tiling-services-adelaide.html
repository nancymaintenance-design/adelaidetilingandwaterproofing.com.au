Tile Preparation and Installation Planning

A tile finish depends on the base, material supply and the order of connected work. Use this guide to prepare for an agreed installation scope, then choose your surface from our tiling services.

Back to tiling services →

Tile removal and substrate preparation

We check the existing finish, base, dimensions, access and adjoining surfaces before agreeing the installation. Tile removal may expose loose adhesive, damaged lining or a base that needs further preparation. The scope identifies removal, levelling or screeding and the accessible repair boundary. Hidden findings are discussed before additional work proceeds.

Moisture, movement and unstable material need assessment before they are covered. Where waterproofing is required, preparation and membrane stages are coordinated with the tiling handover. A new tile finish alone does not renew the concealed membrane.

Tile supply and layout decisions

Confirm whether Ellis or the client supplies the tiles, which format and finish are selected, and when the material will be available. Product dimensions, thickness, edges and intended use affect cuts, transitions and the preparation needed. Share product information before the installation approach is finalised.

Set-out brings adjoining surfaces together. We agree visible cuts, corners, trim positions and movement junctions with the selected tile. Check material responsibility, waste handling and protection of retained finishes in the written quote.

Installation sequence and connected trades

Removal and base preparation come before the agreed membrane or installation stages. Plumbing, electrical, cabinetry or structural changes need their own confirmed responsibilities and access. Install tiles only when the prepared base and any required membrane are ready for the next stage.

Grout and flexible junctions have different roles. Return to use follows the selected materials and site conditions rather than one universal number of hours. Handover explains care, cleaning and any access restrictions. Read the drying and curing guide for the distinct waiting stages.

Prepare for the written scope

  • Surface dimensions, current finish and access.
  • Removal, substrate preparation and hidden findings.
  • Tile supply, product details, layout and edge finishes.
  • Connected trades, waste handling and return to use.
