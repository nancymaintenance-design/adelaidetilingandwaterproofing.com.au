Kitchen Splashback Tiling

A kitchen splashback needs a sound wall, a considered tile layout and suitable junctions around the benchtop and fittings. Ellis assesses these details before quoting your splashback installation, so the preparation, cuts and finished edges are clear before work starts.

Splashback wall preparation and benchtop readiness

Before setting out, we check the existing wall finish, accessible lining, flatness and signs of moisture or damage. Removing old tiles can leave adhesive or damaged plaster that needs preparation. Tiling over an existing finish is conditional on its condition and compatibility; a neat surface alone does not show that it is a suitable base.

Benchtops and fixed cabinetry need to be in their agreed positions. Confirm what stays, what is being replaced and who makes the wall ready after other work. This service covers the tiled splashback rather than a full kitchen renovation. For kitchen floors, see floor tile installation and transitions.

Splashback tile layout, cuts and exposed edges

As your kitchen splashback tiler in Adelaide, we plan the starting line, grout joints and cut positions around windows, cabinetry, corners and outlets. Tile dimensions and patterns affect where narrow cuts fall, how corners meet and how much material is needed. Discuss the visible set-out before ordering and installation.

  • Confirm the tiled height, width and termination points.
  • Agree outlet cutouts, corner treatment and exposed edge trims.
  • Confirm tile supply, quantities, grout colour and finish.
  • Identify protection and access around retained cabinetry and appliances.

Electrical outlets must be made safe and handled by an appropriate licensed electrician. Gas and plumbing changes need the relevant licensed trade. Tile cuts around a fitting do not include disconnecting, moving or reconnecting it; agree that coordination separately.

Benchtop tile joint sealing and replacement gaps

The wall-to-benchtop junction needs a suitable flexible joint detail where movement is expected. Rigid grout is not interchangeable with flexible sealant at that connection. The joint must be clean, prepared and compatible with the adjoining materials; applying a new bead over a failed or contaminated joint can leave the cause unresolved.

If a new benchtop leaves a gap beneath the old splashback, assess its size, exposed wall and surrounding tile condition before choosing a finish. A gap may need lining repair, a tile adjustment or a planned replacement area rather than simply a thicker sealant bead. We agree the visible result and limits before work begins.

Choosing tiles behind the sink and cooktop

Behind a sink, consider splashing, cleaning and the condition of the wall and benchtop connection. Behind a cooktop, the appliance instructions, heat exposure, wall construction and proposed tile, adhesive and joint materials must be checked together. Material suitability depends on the actual installation; there is no single clearance that suits every cooktop or wall system.

Tell us the appliance model and whether it or the cabinetry is changing. Resolve required clearances and separate trade work before finalising the tiled area. Where moisture damage suggests a wider issue, kitchen waterproofing assessment is a separate consideration.

Splashback quote inclusions and kitchen access

Removal, lining repairs, layout complexity, trims and access around retained fittings affect the quote. An occupied kitchen may need temporary restrictions while materials set or cure. Agree access to the sink and cooking area, appliance work and the return-to-use instructions for the selected materials.

Use the guide to comparing tiling quotes to check the preparation and finish, and the drying and curing guide to understand different waiting stages. General material questions are in the tiling FAQs.

Discuss splashback preparation, layout and materials

Tell us whether you are replacing an old splashback or tiling a prepared wall, which benchtop and appliances will remain, and any tile size or pattern already selected. We check the wall, layout and included preparation before confirming a written quote. Photos and measurements are optional; you can enquire before buying tiles.
