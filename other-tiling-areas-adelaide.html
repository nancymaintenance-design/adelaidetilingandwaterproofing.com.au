Other wall and floor tiling areas

For a laundry, entry or another tiled area, tell us the surface, its condition and how the space is used. We assess whether the job is a new tiled finish, retiling or a smaller repair, then set out the preparation and installation required.

Internal floor work, including connected-room layout and floor height transitions, is explained on our floor tiling page. A laundry with wet-area requirements also needs the relevant laundry waterproofing scope considered before finishes are installed.

Worn joints do not always mean the whole surface needs retiling. For shower joints, compare shower regrouting and silicone resealing; for loose or cracked tiles, start with local tile repair. Recurring moisture needs its source assessed, not covered with a new finish.

Room use, surface condition and preparation

  • Room or outdoor location and wall or floor area.
  • Loose tiles, damaged joints, moisture or an unfinished base.
  • Existing and proposed finishes at doors and fittings.
  • Access, material responsibility and any separate trade work.

Photos are useful but optional. You can enquire without measurements or a final tile selection.

Small tiling jobs outside the named room services

We install and repair tiles in entries, utility spaces and other small wall or floor areas. Tell us how you use the space so we can assess installation, local replacement or joint renewal. Specialist stonework and structural repairs require a separate scope; pool-interior enquiries follow our pool waterproofing service.

Moisture beneath tiles and preparation boundaries

A damp patch beneath loose tiles needs its source assessed before a new finish is agreed. We check the accessible base and explain whether removal or further investigation forms part of the proposed work. The size of a patch alone does not determine preparation. Material supply, cuts into retained finishes and access restrictions are confirmed in the written quote.

Small porcelain and ceramic installations beyond the main rooms

We handle small wall and floor tiling projects in laundries, entryways and adjoining utility spaces. Porcelain floor tiles, ceramic wall tiles and feature pieces need the same attention to the base and finished edges as a larger room. We plan cuts around fittings and transitions to existing surfaces.

Tell us where the tiles will go, their dimensions and whether old tiles need removal. A product photo or link helps us discuss the material and intended finish without asking you to identify the installation system yourself.

Small-area tile preparation and finished transitions

  1. Assess the accessible surface, its condition and the intended finish.
  2. Confirm preparation, material responsibility, boundaries and access in a written scope.
  3. Prepare the base and complete the agreed installation or joint renewal, coordinating connected work.
  4. Explain cleaning, curing and return to use at handover. Timing follows the selected materials and site conditions.
