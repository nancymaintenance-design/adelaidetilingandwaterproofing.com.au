Laundry waterproofing around floor wastes and wall junctions

Waterproofing for laundry floors, wall junctions and moisture-exposed surfaces around appliances and fixtures.

We inspect the laundry base, floor waste where present, drainage falls, wall-floor junctions and pipe penetrations. A washing machine hose leak is different from failed floor waterproofing: the water source needs attention before a membrane repair is selected. Plumbing repairs are separate trade work.

Tell us whether water appears during appliance use or at other times, whether the laundry is still in use and whether appliances can be moved. Confirm who disconnects and reinstates them and whether the base needs opening. Your quote identifies removal, substrate preparation, the wet-area membrane boundary and the sequence before the tiled finish.

Included in your agreed scope

  • Preparation of the agreed floor and wall areas
  • Junction and penetration detailing
  • Membrane installation and surface finishing

Appliance leaks and moisture beneath the laundry finish

A supply hose, drain connection or appliance fault can release water onto a sound floor. That source requires separate plumbing or appliance attention; a new membrane does not repair the connection. Note whether dampness appears during filling, draining or while the machine is idle. Avoid disconnecting equipment just to collect evidence. We discuss the pattern and inspect accessible surfaces before deciding whether the floor build-up needs opening.

Floor waste junctions, penetrations and equipment access

The laundry floor waste junction, where present, must be considered with surrounding falls, pipe penetrations and the wet area floor wall junction. We assess whether the existing base can support the selected system and how the work joins retained surfaces. Confirm who moves, disconnects and reinstates the washing machine, dryer or tub before work starts. The written quote separates removal and preparation from membrane work and the final tiled finish; concealed damage can change that scope after exposure.

Before your written quote

Share your suburb, the existing surface and when moisture appears. Safe photos and previous reports are useful if available; you can enquire without them.

Access, retained finishes, removal and separate trade requirements are agreed before installation.

Assessment, preparation and handover

  1. Review the affected area and confirm access, the moisture pattern and suitability.
  2. Agree an itemised written quote covering preparation, materials, boundaries, finish and exclusions.
  3. Prepare the accessible base and carry out the agreed work, with checks appropriate to the selected system.
  4. Explain curing, return to use and care at handover. Timing follows the materials and site conditions.
