Before you enquire

Start with the room and the project stage.

Notes, photos, tile samples and simple measurements can be useful when describing a project. They are not required for an initial enquiry.

The questions below explain the information that is useful to share. They do not replace site-specific assessment or technical advice.

What services are listed for Ellis Services Group in Adelaide?

The website lists waterproofing and tiling for Adelaide projects, including wet-area discussions and wall or floor tiling.

Should waterproofing be considered before tiling?

For a wet-area project, waterproofing is considered before tile and surface finishes are installed. The room layout and project sequence are useful context for the enquiry.

What rooms are commonly discussed for waterproofing?

Bathrooms, shower areas, laundries and other wet areas are common starting points. Describe the particular room in your enquiry.

Is bathroom tiling included in the site’s service information?

Yes. The site includes bathroom wall and floor tiling information for Adelaide projects.

Can I ask about a laundry project?

Yes. State that the project is a laundry and include the current stage, visible surfaces and intended tile finish if known.

What about a kitchen splashback?

The tiling information includes kitchen splashbacks and selected tiled surfaces. The wall area, tile sample and status of cabinetry are useful details to share.

Should I choose a tile before making contact?

No. You can include a tile sample or style direction if you have one, but an initial enquiry can simply identify the room and intended finish.

What is useful to know about the existing surface?

Describe whether the surface is newly prepared, already tiled, or part of an ongoing renovation. A clear photo can add useful context.

Are wall tiles and floor tiles discussed differently?

They can be. Identify the surface, the room, the tile size or sample if known, and whether waterproofing is part of the same project.

Do I need exact measurements before contacting Ellis?

No. Approximate dimensions are helpful if available, but the room type and a short project description are enough for an initial enquiry.

What photos are useful to include?

Wide photos showing the room, plus closer views of the surface or area you want to discuss, give more context than a single cropped image.

What should I write in the enquiry description?

Include the room, whether the project concerns waterproofing, tiling or both, the current stage, and the finish you are considering.

What should I choose under service type?

Choose Waterproofing, Tiling, or Waterproofing and tiling according to the scope you want to describe. If you are unsure, select the closest option and add context in the message field.

Why does the contact form ask for a suburb or property address?

This field gives location context for an Adelaide enquiry. A suburb is sufficient when you do not want to enter a full address.

Can I use the form without a tile sample?

Yes. A tile sample is optional. The room type, surface and project stage are useful even when tile selection is still being considered.

Can I call instead of using the enquiry form?

Can I email instead of using the enquiry form?

The form prepares an email draft addressed to Ellis Services Group in your email application. Review the draft before sending it.
