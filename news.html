Useful questions to consider before work starts.

These guides are general information, not a substitute for advice on a particular site. They explain the kind of detail that helps when describing a bathroom, laundry, kitchen or other tiled area.

Waterproofing

When should waterproofing be considered?

Raise waterproofing early, before tile and surface finishes are installed. The room layout, fixtures, drains and intended finish all provide helpful context for the discussion.

Bathrooms

Why planning matters in bathroom waterproofing

A bathroom has several elements that meet in one space. Discussing the layout and project sequence early helps clarify what needs attention before the finished tile work begins.

Tiling

What to share before starting a tiling project

Room type, tile choice, existing surfaces and timing are all useful. A tile sample or a few clear photos can make the first conversation much easier.

Explore tiling →

Project notes

Keep the brief practical.

For a new bathroom or laundry, it can help to note the proposed fixtures, whether the room is already stripped back, and what happens next in the renovation sequence. For a kitchen splashback, identify the wall area, tile style and whether cabinetry or appliances are already in place.

The aim is not to solve every question before you call. It is simply to give the conversation enough context to be useful from the first contact.
