Bathroom waterproofing–tiling sequence and curing

We coordinate waterproofing and tiling in the right sequence for bathroom renovations. Include the tile type, approximate area and existing surface condition with your enquiry.

Ellis coordinates the membrane and tile work in one service sequence. We plan preparation, curing and installation together, protecting the waterproofing before the new finish is laid.

The sequence on a typical Adelaide bathroom job

  1. Strip-out of existing tiles, bed and fittings.
  2. Substrate preparation and screed to falls, so water moves toward the waste.
  3. Primer applied to the prepared substrate.
  4. Bond breaker and flashing tape at all junctions, corners and penetrations.
  5. Membrane applied in coats, with reinforcement at the junctions.
  6. Cure time, as specified by the membrane manufacturer.
  7. Required inspections or testing for the selected system and project.
  8. Tiling and grouting once curing and required readiness checks are complete.
  9. Silicone at flexible joints — not grout, which cracks at any change of plane.

Bathroom waterproofing FAQs

Can you waterproof over existing tiles?

It depends on the selected system and the condition of the existing tiles and substrate. Waterproofing over tiles does not repair a failed concealed membrane. We inspect the area on site and specify any tile removal and preparation needed in the repair plan and written quote.

How long does bathroom waterproofing take?

We set out the preparation, membrane application, curing and tiling schedule in your quote. The area, substrate condition and selected materials determine the programme, and tiling follows the required readiness checks.

Can I use the bathroom during the work?

The affected bathroom area stays out of use during preparation, installation and curing. We confirm access arrangements and when the completed surfaces are ready for use.

Do I need to replace the whole bathroom?

Our team checks the seals, grout, drainage and adjoining surfaces on site. We identify the water-entry cause and confirm whether the work needs a targeted joint repair or renewal of the affected waterproofing.

What if the leak is already showing downstairs?

Stop using the affected shower and contact Ellis Services Group. We inspect the shower and the affected area below, trace the water-entry cause and confirm the repair plan and quote before retiling.
