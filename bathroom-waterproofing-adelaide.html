Bathroom waterproofing and shower leak repairs

We install and renew waterproofing for shower recesses, bathrooms and ensuites. For damp walls, carpet wet after shower use or staining below a bathroom, we inspect the water-entry cause and complete the targeted repair or membrane renewal required.

  • Shower floors, bathroom floors and wall junctions.
  • Corners, thresholds, wastes and fixture penetrations.
  • Tile removal and substrate preparation within the agreed scope.
  • Membrane installation, required curing and readiness checks.
  • Coordinated tile, grout and flexible-joint finishing.

One coordinated repair and tiled finish

We inspect the existing base and drainage, prepare the affected areas and carry out the specified waterproofing before the bathroom tiled finish. The chosen system and site condition guide the membrane installation, curing and any inspections or testing required.

For worn grout or split seals, see our tile regrouting and shower resealing services. For a full strip-out or changed layout, use our bathroom renovation waterproofing service.

Your bathroom waterproofing quote

Your quote identifies the work area, removal, substrate repairs, membrane installation, materials, access and finishing required. We inspect and measure the room on site, then confirm inclusions, timing and the agreed checks before work.
