Bathroom waterproofing Adelaide

Plan bathroom waterproofing before the tiled finish.

Bathroom waterproofing is best considered alongside the layout, shower area, existing surfaces and planned tile finish. Ellis Services Group discusses wet-area projects with Adelaide property owners before the work reaches the finishing stage.

Start with the bathroom layout and project stage

Every bathroom project is at a different point. Some rooms are being renovated from the substrate, while others have existing finishes that need to be assessed before the next step is clear. Tell us which parts of the bathroom are involved, whether a shower or other wet area is included, and what work has already been completed.

This focused page is for bathroom projects. For a wider overview of wet-area planning, see our waterproofing Adelaide information. If you are also deciding on finished wall or floor surfaces, read the related tiling Adelaide page.

Coordinate waterproofing and tiling in the right order

Waterproofing and tiling are closely connected, but they are not the same part of the project. The sequence matters because the underlying surfaces and the planned finish both affect the discussion. Identifying the tile type, the approximate area and the condition of existing surfaces helps prevent the enquiry from being reduced to a generic request.

Questions to prepare for an enquiry

Before contacting us, note the room size, whether the bathroom is currently in use, the location of the shower and other wet areas, and whether the project is a renovation or a repair. Photos of the current space can help explain the situation. We will discuss the information available and the appropriate scope rather than promise a solution without understanding the room.
