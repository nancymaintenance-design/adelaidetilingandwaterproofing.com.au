Match the membrane, substrate and exposure

We select the waterproofing system around your bathroom, laundry, balcony, roof or pool. Under-tile, exposed and permanently immersed areas need different product suitability checks. We coordinate the primer, membrane, corners, penetrations and compatible finish as a complete build-up.

Your written quote identifies surface preparation, membrane coverage, junction details and any protective finish or tiling included. We confirm separate plumbing work and trade handovers before work begins. Photos are optional: handyman.lyric@outlook.com.
