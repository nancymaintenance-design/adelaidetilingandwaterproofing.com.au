Waterproofing Adelaide

Waterproofing for Adelaide wet areas and finished surfaces.

Ellis Services Group provides waterproofing services for Adelaide bathrooms, laundries, balconies, roofs, kitchens, pools and external surfaces. Send the work area, current condition and required finish in your enquiry.

Bathroom and shower areas

We provide professional bathroom waterproofing for shower areas, wet areas and bathroom surfaces. Ellis Services Group assesses the current room condition and completes the waterproofing work required before the tiled finish. For this specific service, see bathroom waterproofing Adelaide.

Laundry and utility areas

We waterproof laundry floors, wall junctions and wet-area surfaces around appliances and fixtures. Send photos and approximate dimensions so we can assess the work area and provide the right waterproofing service.

Roof waterproofing

Ellis Services Group provides professional roof waterproofing for roof surfaces, junctions and penetrations. We assess the roof damage, exposed areas and water-entry points, then carry out the repair work required.

Kitchen waterproofing

We provide kitchen waterproofing for moisture-exposed walls, floors and wet areas. Our service is coordinated with cabinetry, appliances and the final kitchen surface work.

Pool waterproofing

For pool waterproofing, include the pool-related surface or surrounding area, the current condition and the finish required. Include any edges, junctions or moisture concerns in your enquiry.

External wall, edge and corner waterproofing

For external walls, edges and corners, describe the junction or exposed connection point, the condition you can see and the intended surface work. Clear photos and approximate dimensions help explain the area.

Balconies and external wet-exposed areas

For balconies and other exposed areas, identify the surface, edges or junctions, the existing condition and how the area is used. Mention visible moisture concerns, drainage-related details or surface changes so we can respond to the requested waterproofing service.

Waterproofing and tiling, delivered as one service plan

We coordinate waterproofing with the final wall and floor finish. Our tiling services complete the finished-surface work for bathrooms, kitchens and other Adelaide spaces.
