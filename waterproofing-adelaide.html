Waterproofing repairs for leaks, damp and lifting tiles

Book an assessment if you notice any of these issues. We inspect the affected area and establish the repair required:

  • Grout that keeps crumbling or powdering after repeated re-grouting.
  • Silicone that repeatedly lifts or peels away at junctions.
  • Efflorescence — white, powdery deposits along grout lines.
  • A musty or damp smell that does not clear with ventilation.
  • Staining, bubbling paint or swollen plaster on a ceiling or wall below.
  • Skirting boards or adjacent cabinetry that have swollen or discoloured.
  • Tiles that sound hollow over a widening area, or that start to lift.

Leave investigation of concealed or elevated areas to our team. Do not remove tiles, cut into walls or climb onto a roof to investigate; contact Ellis to arrange a safe on-site assessment.

Waterproofing FAQs

Is waterproofing required before tiling a wet area?

We install the specified wet-area waterproofing before the tiled finish, with surface preparation, drainage and junction details coordinated as part of the service. Tiles and grout alone do not provide the waterproofing layer.

How long does a waterproofing membrane last?

We assess the accessible membrane, substrate and moisture-affected surfaces, then recommend the preparation or renewal needed for the property. The installed system, site conditions and use determine maintenance and service life.

Can waterproofing be done without removing tiles?

Ellis Services Group checks the tiled surface, joints and substrate on site. We confirm whether a local repair suits the findings and identify any tile removal required in the repair plan and written quote.

What do you provide at waterproofing handover?

We explain the completed waterproofing work and provide care, curing and return-to-use instructions for the installed materials. Your agreed scope and handover records identify the work delivered.
