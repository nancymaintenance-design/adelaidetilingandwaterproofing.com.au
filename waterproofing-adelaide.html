Waterproofing Adelaide

Waterproofing for Adelaide wet areas and finished surfaces.

Ellis Services Group helps Adelaide property owners plan waterproofing work before tiles and other finished surfaces are installed. A clear discussion at the right stage can make it easier to coordinate the room, the surfaces and the finish.

Where waterproofing planning matters

Bathrooms, laundries and other wet areas need attention before the visible finish goes on. The room layout, existing surface, drainage details and project stage can all affect how the work is scoped. Tell us whether the area is being renovated, repaired or prepared for a new finish so the conversation starts with the right information.

For a bathroom-specific discussion, visit our bathroom waterproofing Adelaide page. Where tile selection or finished wall and floor surfaces are part of the project, our tiling Adelaide service information explains the related planning questions.

Prepare useful information before you enquire

A few practical details help make an initial discussion more useful: the room or area involved, whether existing tiles or surfaces are still in place, the current stage of the project, and any visible moisture concern. Photos can also help describe the space. We can then discuss the service scope and an appropriate next step without making assumptions about work that has not been inspected.

Waterproofing and the final finish

Waterproofing is part of a wider sequence rather than an isolated decision. Preparation, waterproofing and tiling or other finishing work need to be considered in the right order. If your project includes multiple spaces, identify the priority area first and use the enquiry to explain the intended finish and timing.
