Surface & finish

Consider the whole space, not only the tile.

Tile format, finish and layout all influence the feeling of a room. A splashback may call for a different approach from a bathroom floor, while a tiled wall has different visual and practical considerations from a high-traffic internal floor.

When you get in touch, tell us about the space, the existing substrate, the tiles you have chosen if any, and the result you want the room to achieve. That creates a more useful starting point than choosing from a generic package.

Helpful first details

What to include in your enquiry.

Include the room type, approximate size, whether existing tiles are in place, the tile size or sample if you have one, and whether waterproofing is part of the same project. Photos can also help explain the existing condition of the area.

This information is a practical start for a conversation with Ellis Services Group; project requirements can then be discussed in their own context.
