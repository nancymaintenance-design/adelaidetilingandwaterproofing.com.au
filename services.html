Our services

Waterproofing and tiling services in Adelaide.

Ellis Services Group groups its work into two service categories: Waterproofing and Tiling. Select the closest service area below, then call or send an enquiry with the details of your space.

Service category one

Waterproofing services

Waterproofing is available for roof, wet-area, external and damp-prone locations. Describe the surface, current condition and project stage when contacting Ellis Services Group.

Roof waterproofing

Waterproofing for roof surfaces, junctions, penetrations and other roof areas where water entry is a concern.

Bathroom waterproofing

Waterproofing for bathroom wet areas before tile and finished surfaces are installed.

Kitchen waterproofing

Waterproofing for kitchen areas where the surface and moisture exposure need to be considered.

Balcony waterproofing

Waterproofing for balcony surfaces, edges, corners and drainage-related areas.

Laundry waterproofing

Waterproofing for laundry floors, wall junctions and other wet-area surfaces.

Pool waterproofing

Waterproofing for pool-related surfaces and surrounding areas requiring moisture protection.

External wall, edge and corner waterproofing

Waterproofing for external wall junctions, house edges, corners and exposed connection points.

Other damp areas

Waterproofing for other damp-prone spaces not listed above. Include the area and visible condition in your enquiry.

Service category two

Tiling services

Tiling is available across interior and exterior surfaces. Share the location, existing surface, tile choice if known, and whether waterproofing is part of the same project.

Bathroom tiling

Wall and floor tiling for bathroom spaces, considered alongside the room’s wet-area stage.

Kitchen tiling

Tiling for kitchen splashbacks and selected kitchen wall or floor surfaces.

Balcony tiling

Tiling for balcony surfaces and exterior areas connected to the balcony space.

Room tiling

Tiling for internal rooms where a finished tiled floor or wall surface is planned.

Courtyard tiling

Tiling for courtyard and outdoor living surfaces.

Wall tiling

Tiling for interior or selected exterior wall surfaces, including feature and practical tiled walls.

Other tiling areas

Tiling for other locations not listed above. Describe the area and desired finished surface in your enquiry.
