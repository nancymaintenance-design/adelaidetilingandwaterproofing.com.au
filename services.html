Choose tiles for your space and plan the installation

We install porcelain and ceramic floor and wall tiles, subway splashbacks, and ceramic or glass mosaics for niches and feature areas. For bathroom floors and pool surrounds, we check the selected product’s wet-use suitability and slip information; for shower walls and kitchen splashbacks, we compare surface texture, water marks, cooking splashes and grout care.

We plan base preparation, tile layout, adhesive coverage, drainage falls and edge details around the actual tile size, thickness and location. Tell us your preferred product and installation area so we can confirm material responsibilities, work sequencing and the scope in your written quote.
