Material selection

Wet-area tile selection and exterior tile suitability

For bathroom and laundry floors, we assess wet-use suitability, tile texture and drainage. Splashbacks need accurate set-out around cabinetry and fittings. Living floors need considered transitions and movement joints; balconies, courtyards and pool surrounds need exterior-suitable tiles and installation materials.

We confirm the tile selection, quantities and whether supply is included in your written quote. Large-format tiles, mosaics, cuts, access and substrate preparation all influence the installation scope and price.

Tile repairs and replacement

We assess loose or damaged tiles and the surface beneath them to determine the repair required. Isolated replacement, regrouting and renewed flexible junctions are scoped around the existing condition and available matching tiles.

For widespread lifting or recurring wet-area damage, we address the underlying preparation or waterproofing as part of the repair rather than simply covering the surface.

Tiling questions

Tile supply, over-tiling and curing: installation answers

Can you tile over existing tiles?

We assess the bond, flatness, substrate condition and finished height. Where the existing surface is suitable, over-tiling can be included in the scope. Where removal is required, we include preparation for the new surface.

Do you supply the tiles?

We can include tile supply in the agreed scope or install your selected tiles. Your quote confirms materials, quantities and responsibilities before work begins.

When can I use the finished area?

We provide return-to-use instructions based on the installed adhesive, grout and waterproofing system, including curing requirements before heavy use or wetting.

What determines the tiling quote?

The area, tile format, cuts and layout, access, removal and preparation requirements determine the scope. We inspect these items and provide a written quote showing the work and materials included.
